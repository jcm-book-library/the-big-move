How this works

Every item has a little box. Tap it to move through the three states:

Not started: still to do.
Stalled: stuck or waiting. Add the reason in the notes.
Done: finished.
  • Next up pulls together what's overdue, due in the next 14 days, high priority with no date, and stalled.
  • Drag the ⠿ grip to reorder. You can also use Move up / Move down in a list's menu, or Move to list inside an item.
  • Tap an item's title to add a due date, notes, a link or a cost.
  • Done lists can be archived from their ⋯ menu, and found again under Archive.
